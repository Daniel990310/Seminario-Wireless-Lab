Beyond Connectivity: Wireless Sensing in mmWave and Sub-THz Bands. 21–22 October 2026, Auditorium, PUCV Santiago Campus, Chile. Organised by: Mauricio Rodríguez, PhD. Communication and sensing over the same infrastructure. Propagation, channel modelling and ISAC architectures at mmWave and sub-THz: 6G networks that carry data and, with the same signal, sense their environment. Scan the code or go to bcsensing.org.
