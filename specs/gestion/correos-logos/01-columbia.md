Dear Sir or Madam,

The Pontificia Universidad Católica de Valparaíso (PUCV), Chile, is organising the
international seminar **Beyond Connectivity: Wireless Sensing in mmWave and Sub-THz
Bands**, to be held on **21–22 October 2026** at our Santiago campus (Antonio Bellet 314,
Providencia, Santiago). Attendance is free of charge. The seminar is funded by the Chilean
National Research and Development Agency (ANID), project **FOVI250222**, and is organised
by our School of Electrical Engineering.

The website is **https://bcsensing.org**.

**Prof. Gil Zussman**, Department of Electrical Engineering, Columbia Engineering (SEAS),
is one of the eight invited speakers.

We are writing to request written permission for two specific things:

1. **To display the Columbia University logo** on the seminar website, in a section listing
   the institutions of the participating researchers, each logo linked to its institution's
   homepage. The logo would be shown unmodified, in the variant your guidelines authorise
   for the background it sits on, with no implication of endorsement or sponsorship by
   Columbia University.
2. **To be pointed to the correct file and to your visual identity manual.** We want to
   flag an error on our side so that you can correct us: the files we currently hold are
   `CUSPS_logo_simple_RGB_*`, which we understand to be the School of Professional Studies
   mark — not the correct one for a SEAS faculty member. **We have not published them.** We
   would rather use the file you tell us is right than the one we happened to find.

Our website offers a light and a dark theme, so we would need the variants authorised for
each background, preferably in SVG. We do not recolour, crop or otherwise alter
institutional marks, which is precisely why we are asking for both variants rather than
adapting one.

If a formal licence or a different process applies, we would be grateful for guidance. **The
section can remain unpublished until you reply** — as of today no third-party mark is shown
on the site; each one displays a placeholder instead.

With thanks and kind regards,

**Daniel Caignet González**
Organising committee, *Beyond Connectivity 2026*

  *«If you would like to request special permission to use the parent brand trademark,
  please email your request to Columbia Creative»* `[verificado: 2026-09-23]`. **Ese es
  exactamente nuestro caso**: un tercero que quiere usar la marca matriz, no una unidad
  interna usando la suya.
  El correo va ofuscado por Cloudflare en esa página; se decodificó con el esquema público
  del propio Cloudflare. **Conviene confirmarlo a ojo en el navegador antes de enviar.**
- `licensing@columbia.edu` va en copia porque el Trademark and Licensing Program es quien
  administra las marcas; la misma página remite al **Office of General Counsel**
  (`ogc.columbia.edu`) si hay dudas legales. Si Columbia Creative deriva, será a uno de
  esos dos.

  —su agente de licencias, `licensing@exemplar.com`—, es porque leyeron «logo» y pensaron
  en productos. Vale aclarar que es una web académica sin fin comercial.

  Si se borra esa parte, lo más probable es que manden el mismo que ya tenemos.
