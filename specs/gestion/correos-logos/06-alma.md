Dear Sir or Madam,

The Pontificia Universidad Católica de Valparaíso (PUCV), Chile, is organising the
international seminar **Beyond Connectivity: Wireless Sensing in mmWave and Sub-THz
Bands**, to be held on **21–22 October 2026** at our Santiago campus (Antonio Bellet 314,
Providencia, Santiago). Attendance is free of charge. The seminar is funded by the Chilean
National Research and Development Agency (ANID), project **FOVI250222**, and is organised
by our School of Electrical Engineering.

The website is **https://bcsensing.org**.

**Giorgio Siringo**, Front-End Technical Lead at the Joint ALMA Observatory, is one of the
eight invited speakers.

Your copyright notice states that the general Creative Commons permission does not extend
to the ALMA logo, which may not be used or reproduced without prior and individual written
consent, and that external websites wishing to link to the ALMA site may use the logo for
that link with prior authorisation from the Education and Public Outreach Department. We
are writing to request exactly that authorisation.

Specifically, we ask for:

1. **Written consent to display the ALMA logo** on the seminar website, in a section listing
   the institutions of the participating researchers. The logo would be shown unmodified and
   **used as a link to the ALMA website**, with no implication of endorsement or sponsorship
   of the seminar by ALMA or by any of its partners (ESO, NRAO, NAOJ).
2. **The authorised file, in the variants for light and dark backgrounds**, preferably in
   SVG. Our site offers both themes and we do not recolour or otherwise alter institutional
   marks, which is why we ask for both variants rather than adapting one.
3. **Any credit line or attribution wording** you require alongside it, and the brand
   guidelines that apply.

We would also be grateful for your guidance on one point: Dr Siringo's affiliation as given
to us by the organisers is "ALMA / European Southern Observatory". If you consider that a
different mark or wording is the correct one for this case, we would rather follow your
guidance than decide it ourselves.

**The section can remain unpublished until you reply** — as of today no third-party mark is
shown on the site; each one displays a placeholder instead.

With thanks and kind regards,
