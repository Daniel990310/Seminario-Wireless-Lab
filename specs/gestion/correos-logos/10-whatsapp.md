Para pedir por chat lo que no se pide por correo: que **alguien haga el envío**. Los dos
son para copiar y pegar tal cual.

Van aquí y no sueltos en una conversación porque, si no está escrito en el repositorio, no
ocurrió — y saber a quién se le pidió qué es la mitad de este trámite.

### Mensaje 2 · el párrafo para que él pegue

```
One more small request: your institution's logo.

The seminar website (https://bcsensing.org) includes a section listing the
institutions of the participating researchers. We do not publish any institutional
mark without permission from its owner, so that section currently shows
placeholders.

If you are able to help, we would be grateful if you could either put us in touch
with the team at your institution that grants permission to use its logo, or
forward us the authorised files yourself if your institution allows it. What we
need is:

- permission to display the logo on the website, linked to your institution's
  homepage, shown unmodified and with no implication of endorsement or sponsorship;
- the authorised file in the variants for light and dark backgrounds — the site has
  both themes and we do not recolour institutional marks — preferably in SVG;
- the brand guidelines that apply.

If your institution has a form or a specific process for this, just pointing us to
it is already a great help.
```
