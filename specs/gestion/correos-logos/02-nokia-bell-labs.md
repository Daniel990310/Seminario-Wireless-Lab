**Asunto:** Written consent to display the Nokia Bell Labs logo — academic seminar website (PUCV, Chile)

Dear Sir or Madam,

The Pontificia Universidad Católica de Valparaíso (PUCV), Chile, is organising the
international seminar **Beyond Connectivity: Wireless Sensing in mmWave and Sub-THz
Bands**, to be held on **21–22 October 2026** at our Santiago campus (Antonio Bellet 314,
Providencia, Santiago). Attendance is free of charge. The seminar is funded by the Chilean
National Research and Development Agency (ANID), project **FOVI250222**, and is organised
by our School of Electrical Engineering.

The website is **https://bcsensing.org**.

Two of the eight invited speakers are researchers at Nokia Bell Labs: **Dr Jinfeng Du** and
**Dr Reinaldo A. Valenzuela**.

We note that Nokia's terms of use state that access to the website grants no licence or
right to use any Nokia trademark. We are therefore requesting **prior written consent** to
display the Nokia Bell Labs logo on the seminar website, in a section listing the
institutions of the participating researchers, linked to bell-labs.com, shown unmodified,
and with no suggestion of sponsorship or endorsement by Nokia.

We would also be grateful to receive:

- **the brand guidelines applicable to this use**, and
- **the authorised file in the variants for light and dark backgrounds**, preferably in
  SVG. Our site offers both themes and we do not alter marks to fit a background, which is
  why we ask for both rather than adapting one.

If consent must be granted through a specific process or by a different team, please let us
know and we will follow it. **The section can remain unpublished until then** — as of today
no third-party mark is shown on the site; each one displays a placeholder instead.

Kind regards,

**Daniel Caignet González**
Organising committee, *Beyond Connectivity 2026*
Escuela de Ingeniería Eléctrica, Pontificia Universidad Católica de Valparaíso
